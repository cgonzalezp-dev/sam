If SLA is green but CSAT is falling, I don't push productivity. I run the structure.

  • Segment the feedback — never react to one aggregate number.
  • Diagnose the real driver: process, product, staffing, or agent behavior.
  • Prioritize by impact and effort — fix what moves the business first.
  • Execute a cross-functional action plan with owners and dates.
  • Measure against a defined outcome — and make the fix repeatable, not a one-off.

Four values I actually operate by — the ones that make an exigent standard and genuine care live in the same room.

01

Kindness is a leadership skill

Too much energy is lost to unmet expectations and judgment. I start from open, humble kindness — toward the team and the client — and more gets done.

02

Invest in your people

The best return on time is developing others. I build learning programs and tools myself, because a good quarter doesn't guarantee a strong team next year.

03

Trust is earned, not claimed

With a client and with a team, credibility comes from consistency and delivery — one business review, one commitment kept, at a time.

04

Plan deliberately, act decisively

Strategy sets up the win; urgency demands a call. I plan so that when the moment comes, the fast decision is already an informed one.
